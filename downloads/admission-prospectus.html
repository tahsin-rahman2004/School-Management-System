S. M. Pilot Model School

Admission overview

  • Class 1: digital lottery, birth certificate, photograph and guardian details. No previous academic certificate.
  • Classes 2–9: subject to vacancies, written admission exam, transfer certificate and previous academic certificate/report card.
  • Class 10: check vacancies and contact the school office. No online application.

Written exams

Classes 2–5: 1 hour, 50 marks (Bangla 15, English 15, Mathematics 20). Classes 6–9: 2 hours, 100 marks (Bangla 30, English 30, Mathematics 40).

Age and documents

Follow the supplied 2027 policy and school-confirmed cut-off dates. Class 1 has an age 6+ target within the policy’s 5–7 range. Higher-class ages follow progressively; special-needs provisions apply as specified in the policy.

Prepare birth registration evidence, photographs, guardian information and relevant supporting documents. Dates and fees will be announced by the school.
