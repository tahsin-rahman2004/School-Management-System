# School website

Open `t.html` to view the website. The menu links to the gallery, academic calendar and results portal.

## Academic calendar

`academic-calendar.html` includes month navigation, event filters and exam date ranges. Full cells use red for holidays, purple for exams, green for activities and blue for academic events. Exam ranges skip Friday and Saturday closures.

Dates and closures are illustrative. Update them in `script.js` using the school's approved schedule. Shared styling is in `style.css`.

## Results portal

Open `results.html` or choose Results from the menu. Use **View sample marksheet**, or search with:

- Academic year: 2026
- Class: 8
- Examination: Annual Examination
- Roll: 12

The sample marksheet includes subject grades, total marks and GPA, PDF download and printing. The page also has publication information, an achievements section and an official Education Board results link.

Sample reference: `SAMPLE-2026-8-12`. Reference checking identifies the demo only; it does not authenticate official records.

Edit `results.html`, `results.css` and `results.js` for the results portal. Real student authentication, marks upload, headteacher approval and publication require a backend.
