About our school

Rooted in Bancharampur

Founded in 1948, S. M. Pilot Model School is part of the community in Bancharampur, Brahmanbaria. Today, our school welcomes learners from Class 1 to Class 10.

Our gallery records students and teachers coming together for sports, award presentations and scouting activities—moments of school life beyond the classroom. ( See the gallery → )

Our former students are spread across the world. Through further study, their careers and their communities, they carry the memories and connections formed at this school into new places and new chapters of life.

History & heritage

Established in 1948, the school grew from a local effort to expand education in Bancharampur, Brahmanbaria. It began as Bancharampur S. M. High School, supported by local education-minded residents.

The school was nationalised on 25 September 2018. Alongside classroom learning, its history highlights participation in sports and cultural activities at upazila, district and national levels. Former students have continued their studies and careers in Bangladesh and abroad.

Messages from school leadership

Chairperson’s message

Ferdous Ara

Chairperson · Upazila Nirbahi Officer, Bancharampur

Education is part of life itself. Alongside academic learning, the school aims to support students’ personal development, creativity and confidence, helping them discover their abilities.

A supportive learning environment brings together study, sports, cultural activities and co-curricular opportunities. The message emphasises developing students’ physical and mental abilities through participation and care.

Head teacher’s message

Mohammad Atikur Rahman

Head Teacher

Science and information technology have an important place in education. The school’s website is intended to connect students, teachers, guardians and former students with information about learning, examinations, results and school activities.

The message calls for cooperation from teachers, students, guardians and the wider community to help learners become knowledgeable, responsible citizens and contribute to the country’s development.
