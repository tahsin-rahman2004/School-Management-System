Classes, ages & available seats

Admission is subject to the official school notice. Confirm class availability and age cut-off with the office.

Age eligibility (supplied 2027 policy)

Class 1: the policy targets age 6+, with a minimum age of 5 on 1 January and an upper age limit of 7 in the admission year. Higher-class ages follow progressively from Class 1. Students with special needs may receive up to 5 additional years under the policy. Birth-registration evidence is required; the school will confirm exact cut-off dates. Class 10 transfer eligibility is checked separately.

How to apply

  1. Check the admission notice and eligibility.
  2. Complete the application and prepare documents.
  3. Submit your application online when available, or hand it in at the school office.
  4. Class 1 uses a digital lottery; Classes 2–9 require a written exam. For Class 10, check vacancies and contact the school office.

Documents to prepare

  • Birth registration certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Classes 2–9: previous academic certificate/report card; required transfer certificate. Class 1: no previous academic certificate required.
  • Parent/guardian names, phone, address and NID copies as required by the school.

Lottery & written admission exam

Class 1: digital lottery, no admission exam. Classes 2–5: a 1-hour written exam, 50 marks (Bangla 15, English 15, Mathematics 20). Classes 6–9: a 2-hour written exam, 100 marks (Bangla 30, English 30, Mathematics 40). Class 10: contact the school office if seats are available. Dates will be announced by the school.

Fees, scholarships & waivers

Frequently asked questions

Can my child apply mid-year?

Contact the office to check vacancies and transfer rules. Mid-year entry is not guaranteed.

Is there a waiting list?

Ask the admission office whether a waiting list is offered for your chosen class.

Do you offer scholarships?

Available support and eligibility must be confirmed with the school office.

How will I hear from the school?

The school office will email eligible applicants about admission or the admission test after reviewing submitted applications. You may submit your documents at the school office too.

Transport & facilities

Ask the school about current library and laboratory access, sports facilities, transport availability, routes and charges before making arrangements.
